## Pre-launch: dry run on the four Fridays before launch (not part of the track record)

I ran `live.py` on real CBOE prices from 23 October to 20 November 2026, to test the code.

| Friday | UX1 | UX2 | UX2 / UX1 - 1 | Signal | Position for Monday | Next week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 23/10/2026 | 19.61 | 19.50 | -0.6% | cash | 0 | 0 |
| 30/10/2026 | 20.36 | 19.79 | -2.8% | cash | 0 | 0 |
| 06/11/2026 | 23.69 | 22.18 | -6.4% | cash | 0 | 0 |
| 13/11/2026 | 19.11 | 19.44 | +1.7% | **short** | -5 contracts Dec 2026 (9% of the NAV) | **-3.7%** |
| 20/11/2026 | 28.46 | 26.25 | -7.8% | cash | 0 | |

**Letter to investors, dry run.** A good first lesson. The curve turns back to contango on
13 November, the book goes short on Monday 16, and the VIX jumps from 19 to 28 during the
week. The position is small (9% of the NAV) because the future is very volatile (109% over
20 days), so the loss stays at 3.7%. On 20 November the curve is in backwardation again
and the book is in cash. The first real position is computed on Friday 27 November and
published on Sunday 29 November.
